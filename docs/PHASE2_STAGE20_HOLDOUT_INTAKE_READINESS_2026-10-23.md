`forecast_holdout_readiness.prepare_holdout_readiness` reuses the established source-bound V1/V2 forecast and outcome reconstructions plus the separate Stage 19 declared-clock audit. It accepts explicit selection and holdout history lists, a declared freeze time and evaluation cutoff. It pins exact forecast identities and separate partition hashes; each case carries asset/class, source, horizon, model/version/configuration, dataset identity/hash, issue/target time, maturity and exclusion reason. Within an asset/source/horizon cohort it refuses model, version, configuration, release-source or dataset-ID substitution. It refuses duplicate forecasts and repeated target observations, selection labels reconciled at or after the freeze, incomplete selection windows, holdout forecasts issued before the freeze, and source/history substitution. Underlying dataset and replay validators continue to reject future input visibility and altered committed revisions. A retrospective forecast artifact is **not** independently time-custodied: the preflight marks freeze and unseen status false and never scores or authenticates it.

The protocol reports `holdout_mature_n` only as a structural count. It does not compute skill, interval coverage, calibration, significance or confidence intervals. Scoring, when independently authorized, must use frozen asset/source/horizon/model cohorts and the existing explicit MAE/MAPE, direction, probability and interval definitions only where their inputs exist. Corrected outcomes retain original forecast identities and must be evaluated under a separately reviewed as-of revision policy; they are not extra forecasts. Pending, immature, partial, missing, contradictory, unverified and publication-order-ambiguous cases are excluded. Repeated targets are not independent samples. The authoritative roadmap does not specify a universal AFI sample minimum or performance threshold; `proposed_minimum_samples` is a bounded **proposal**, `sample_minimum_approved` remains false, and the independent research reviewer must approve cohort-specific adequacy, uncertainty/interval method and decision criteria **before** accessing the holdout. A caller-retained local digest detects accidental substitution but does not prove external custody or a true pre-outcome freeze.

`validate_intake_declaration` defines `AFI_WITNESSED_SOURCE_INTAKE_V1` with provider/instrument/source identity, license ID/digest and exact permitted uses, observation/publication/witness clocks, witness identity and claimed payload digest, receipt ID, revision ancestry, correction publication clock, retention policy and source-completeness declaration. It rejects missing or contradictory fields, rights other than declared offline research, missing witness metadata, impossible clock order and false authentication claims. A structurally valid packet returns `PENDING_INDEPENDENT_AUTHENTICATION` with `license_verified`, `witness_verified`, `source_completeness_verified` and authority all false. Cryptographic signature validation, provider entitlement, independent witness custody, payload-to-receipt binding and actual retention proof are **not implemented or inferred** from a self-supplied envelope. They require a separately trusted verifier and real licensed evidence; no keys or signatures were invented.
