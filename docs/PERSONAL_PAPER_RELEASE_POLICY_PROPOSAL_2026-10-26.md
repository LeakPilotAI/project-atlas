Perpetual1USD modeled risk maximum per trade,three positions,existing3Rsession stop,and all stricter existing leverage/notional/margin/fee/funding/execution constraints. 4. **Exact accounting:**Decimal quantities/cash,basis,costs,funding and realizedP&L;once-per-epoch inception;durable operation identities,intent/COMMIT/ACK,independent arithmetic/replay functions and reports. Here 'independent' means a distinct accounting derivation in code,not a required external employee. No duplicate effects or fake flattening.
5. **Corruption/UNKNOWN:**missing/conflicting records,wrong resource identity,unacknowledged effects,uncertain quantities or bad checksums block new financial effects. Operator Start does not override this. No blind callback replay,reset,in-place history rewrite or old-position erasure.
6. **Local lifecycle:**D:only,existing Windows account,protected Atlas child resources,retained exact owned-process handles,entry admission/drain and no Genesis access. Loopback/Host/origin controls remain. A browser GET cannot start a provider,writer or financial mutation.
7. **Evidence labeling:**historical research stays historical;every current value carries provenance/time/freshness. Observed IEXquotes are not consolidated NBBO;hypothetical fills are SIMULATED. Missing valuation is UNAVAILABLE,never zero. No scanner result or high ranking alone authorizes an otherwise invalid fill.
8. **Execution data:**use valid observed executable prices/sizes and permitted participation;respect sessions,halts,disconnects and data limits. Perpetual mark touch is not maker execution;funding needs appropriate settlement evidence. No real-time signals from stale retained observations. Zero valid trades is a healthy running session.
9. **Pause/Stop/restart:**operator-controlled global Pause/Stop. Normal Stop closes entries,cancels known unfilled simulated entries and attempts fresh-evidence flattening. If closed markets/stale data/UNKNOWN prevents it,preserve positions and show SHUTDOWN_INCOMPLETE;close only owned processes safely. Emergency Stop preserves evidence. Reconcile durable state and obtain fresh quotes on every restart;no automatic reboot resume/deferred trade.
