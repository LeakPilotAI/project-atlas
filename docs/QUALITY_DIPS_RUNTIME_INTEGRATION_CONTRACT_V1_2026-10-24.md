## 2. Four distinct states

1. **Ranked candidate:** retained research observation plus exact board/cohort revision, source cutoffs, strategy policy and ordinal. Missing evidence remains research-only or refused.
2. **Eligible execution instrument:** independent verified listing identity, share units, USD currency, supported long/fractional rules, admitted account policy, fresh accepted quote and current source evidence. A registry `tradable` boolean is insufficient.
3. **Manually approved command:** issuer-verified approval binds account/epoch, operation ID, action, stable instrument ID, quantity, source/position revisions, quote and candidate digests, expiry and single-use nonce. Operator cancellation/dismissal cannot approve. `manual=True` is only an offline field, never native approval.
4. **Durably acknowledged effect:** FS2 committed canonical event+receipt and required FS3 projection acknowledgement, with accepted current financial pin advanced. Commit-before-ack is UNKNOWN, not permission to issue another fill.

Neither a rank change nor an approval supplies missing market evidence. Existing positions may remain overnight and be manually exited without current rank eligibility; identity, fresh price, exact remaining quantity and manual approval still apply.

## 3. Replace disposable ranking pins without rewriting inception

This is the concrete runtime version design, NOT implemented/provisioned in this task:

- Keep V2 frozen-pin inception/history replayable. New operational inception requires an explicitly reviewed schema version binding immutable risk policy, accepted instrument-rule authority identity and ranking-publisher authority descriptor, rather than a growing inception list of caller hashes. No in-place edits or automatic V2 promotion.
- The independently accepted publisher consumes existing immutable observations/board output. It emits append-only `QD_CANDIDATE_REVISION_V1` envelopes: publisher identity, account scope, monotonically increasing generation, predecessor identity/hash, full source/cohort commitments, source timestamps, stable instrument/observation/ladder IDs, ranking algorithm/policy versions, exact ordinal and explanation, execution metadata revision, issued/expiry times and authenticity proof. The proof mechanism and custody must be accepted under D1; no key or issuer is invented here.
- New generations use an accepted protected **current ranking high-water** reference separate from the financial source revision. Publish immutable body, validate it, then durably publish its current reference/ack with trusted custody. An incomplete generation never becomes current. Preserve all older versions. A signature/hash by itself is not freshness, anti-rollback or authority.
- Replacement/invalidation emits a new authenticated revision or tombstone, serialized with financial admission; absence, gaps, stale/replayed generations, unsupported provider corrections, changed instrument identity, expired quotes or changed policy revoke entry eligibility. Missing publisher/current custody => REFUSED. Never fall back to a local caller-created digest or last good expired rank.
- A narrow selected-account dispatcher holds the accepted shared admission/evidence lease while checking current candidate, instrument and quote revisions, consuming manual approval and preparing the financial intent. Publishers/invalidation participate in the same exclusion protocol through commit. If cross-resource exclusion or a required current read cannot be proven, block. Do not claim multi-store atomicity: a durable authorization reservation and canonical operation ID reconcile any partial progress; ambiguous reservation/financial commit remains UNKNOWN. Financial events may not be blindly retried from a lost acknowledgement.
- Freeze the exact verified decision envelope in the canonical event/receipt. Historical replay verifies what was accepted at that decision time, not today's candidate list. Reordering does not mint a new observation+ladder signal. Restart requires trusted current high-water continuity for new writes; locally consistent coherent rollback remains a D1 blocker.
- Trusted UTC/source freshness plus monotonic in-process elapsed checks must be independently accepted. Wall-clock reversal, restart without current evidence, future-dated data or expiry during approval refuse. The current offline <=600s ranking/<=30s quote bounds are proposals requiring actual feed/session acceptance.

## 5. Writer/resource and shutdown acceptance

Selected writer roster: manual dispatcher/canonical publisher; candidate publisher/current-reference custodian; instrument/execution-evidence publisher; quote-evidence publisher; projection publisher; report publisher; approval/admission journal and recovery controller. Register exact resource roles and process incarnation; reject missing/extra/stale writers or unidentified child writers. Resources: disjoint canonical root, projection root, report root, admission/approval root, candidate/instrument/quote evidence roots and independently held current pins. No production paths/IDs provisioned by this document. Windows file identity, protected namespaces/sidecars, ownership and nonparticipant exclusion require C1/D1 evidence; same-user/admin resistance and full-machine rollback limits stay explicit.

Shutdown: close new financial admission; drain already admitted commands with unchanged lifecycle deadline; preserve pending/UNKNOWN; no forced liquidation; finish required projection acknowledgements; independently reconcile positions/cash/fees; publish an immutable accounting report or explicit incomplete result; close final financial/report admission; verify all registered writers and exact owned children quiescent; only then allow separately authorized owned-service shutdown. Legitimate open overnight positions may coexist with clean lifecycle, but unresolved financial commits or incomplete writer evidence may not. Accounting COMPLETE is not clean shutdown. Recovery never clears UNKNOWN or adopts legacy positions automatically.
