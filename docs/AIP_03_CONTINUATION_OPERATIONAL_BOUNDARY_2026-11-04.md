Partial maps, COMMIT without admission ACK, changed current source, missing records, rollback or contradictory custody are UNKNOWN. Original input-revision, source logical/publication revision and native-image revision remain distinct. Deterministic financial/report/order projections resolve full predecessor history. Retained source archives/backups remain non-executable; restored custody or writable selection is not implied by a backup.
