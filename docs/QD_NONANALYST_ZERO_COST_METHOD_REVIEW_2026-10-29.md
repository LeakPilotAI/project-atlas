## Existing contract and actual admission path

`backend/app/investment/quality_dips_v2_valuation.py` explicitly accepts HISTORICAL_MULTIPLE, SECTOR_RELATIVE, DCF, OWNER_EARNINGS and MANUAL_RESEARCH in addition to ANALYST_CONSENSUS. These labels accept *already supplied* positive finite estimates; none selects or calculates a valuation model. Inputs are `value` (or legacy `target`/`fair_value` alias), provenance/method, original `as_of`, optional confidence/note. Current explicit research applies the existing seven-day valuation TTL, rejects unavailable/non-FRESH flags, undated/naive/future/stale sources and nonfinite/bool values. Historical display compatibility is separate from current acceptance.

At least three valid explicit estimates form sorted low / upper-middle / high normalization values. There is no requirement that these be analyst targets, three independent analysts, three methods or three providers. Equally, the formatter does **not** certify a method's historical coverage, model arithmetic, currency/share basis or dispersion. A method label alone is not evidence. The new method's evidence must be verified before it is offered to this existing formatter, and the native current-source authority must bind the result.

The resulting values travel through `quality_dips_v2_board.build_v2_projection` to `quality_dips_v3.build_v3_entry_plan`. V3's median anchor,15/20/25/30% margin levels, quality>=75, fundamentals>=70, valuation>=50, intact thesis and MEDIUM/HIGH evidence remain unchanged. Deeper classifications retain stricter quality/fundamental/valuation/drawdown requirements. The separate V2 research gate, legacy board's scoring/blockers and original research completeness remain applicable. `quality_dips_candidate_evidence.CandidateEvidenceAdapter.candidate` additionally requires a current accepted ranking revision, complete eligible original observation, matching V3 ladder/reached level, current instrument/execution evidence and no row/missing-critical blockers.

**Non-analyst estimates can satisfy the normalization input, but cannot by themselves authorize ranking or entry.** Test fixtures confirm the same V3 anchor and thresholds apply to each of the five allowed non-analyst labels. No analyst-target fields need be manufactured: future outputs would use `valuation_sources` with their actual method, leaving `target_low_price`/`target_mean_price`/`target_high_price` unavailable.

The existing ordinal `scoring.score_valuation` is a *different* mechanism. It scores observed current ratios in correlated groups using existing bands, not target prices or historical-percentile fair values. Adding three scenarios must not inflate those groups or rewrite that score. Three copies of an earnings model are correlated scenarios, not three independent supporting research pillars. Fundamentals, thesis, risk, history, execution and account limits remain separate.

His [cash-flow definitions](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/dcfcf.pdf) explicitly account for reinvestment and net borrowing in FCFE. These sources support the limitations, **not** the numerical guardrails proposed by Atlas below. No NYU dataset or third-party multiple was ingested or treated as an entitled feed.

## Exact proposal requiring the sole operator's decision

Proposed recipe identifier: **`QD_HISTORICAL_EARNINGS_MULTIPLE_V1`**. Proposed permission: **source-only implementation, public-data feasibility verification and disposable tests**, with no financial activation or startup. It would add a current internally calculated relative-valuation research method, preserving V3/risk/accounting semantics and all analyst field meanings.

The following are **proposed policy choices, not existing accepted rules or empirically proven safe investment thresholds**:

1. Start with operating-company common stocks with verified SEC CIK, Alpaca asset/security class and USD/share basis. No automatic ETF, preferred-share, fund, financial-firm or REIT fallback. Applicable industry/type evidence must be explicit; missing classification does not imply an operating company. Those exclusions narrow this method only; they do not remove instruments from Atlas's research universe or alter other accepted methods.
2. Use **four consecutive, directly reported standalone quarterly diluted EPS observations** in a consistent share basis. Their sum is explicitly labelled `SUM_OF_REPORTED_QUARTERLY_DILUTED_EPS`, a TTM proxy rather than a claim of exact reported annual weighted-share EPS. Do not difference annual/YTD EPS, infer missing Q4, infer weighted-average shares from period-end shares, or silently replace common EPS with total net income. Require latest period and positive current proxy; original signed losses remain intact. This proxy definition itself requires approval.
3. Reconstruct up to five years of **distinct quarterly observation anchors**, each at the first completed regular IEX session whose UTC date is strictly after every contributing filing's `filed` date. DAY precision is not an invented intraday acceptance time. Only facts available by that cut may contribute. Amendments/restatements must not backfill later values into earlier cuts. Require at least **12 distinct complete anchors spanning at least33months**; gaps or missing quarters refuse the method rather than reuse one annual value. This is a research reconstruction, not independently authenticated proof that Atlas possessed those data historically or a backtest acceptance.
4. For each historical anchor, compute `M_t = raw IEX close_t / positive TTM EPS proxy_t`. Require exact currency, issuer, session and per-share basis. Never divide a dividend-adjusted `adjustment=all` price by nominal reported EPS. Explicit split/corporate-action/filing-version reconciliation is mandatory; missing share-basis evidence refuses. If needed, add only the documented Alpaca IEX **split-only historical-bar adjustment** to the existing read-only allowlist after approval; this does not grant a different feed, order route or subscription. Split-only series alone is not proof that a filing's EPS is in the matching share basis.
5. Require every retained multiple in **[5,60]** and every historical/current TTM proxy positive. Refuse on an out-of-range anchor or missing coverage; do not silently trim, winsorize, replace negative earnings, or optimize the bounds to force eligibility. These are proposed conservative exclusion rules, not assertions that every valid company must trade within this range.
6. Use exact Decimal arithmetic and nearest-rank percentiles of the same verified cohort: **25th/50th/75th** with index `ceil(p*n)-1`. The three values are `current TTM EPS proxy * corresponding historical multiple`. Reject if optimistic/conservative **>2**, or any result is nonpositive/nonfinite/contradictory. Do not clamp extreme dispersion into a qualifying result. With only12quarterly anchors the estimates are sparse and correlated; report the sample count, period and full sensitivity explicitly.
7. Label all three `HISTORICAL_MULTIPLE` with recipe version, security/CIK/currency/share basis, input/filing hashes/accessions and original dates, anchor/session evidence, actual cohort and percentile rule. `as_of` is the calculation cut, but cannot renew stale underlying financials. Current fact freshness remains45days, computed-estimate freshness at most7days, current reference-price/execution freshness remains governed separately. No missing earnings/FCF/history/source is replaced with zero. Negative FCF remains visible and subject to unchanged thesis/risk rules, never converted to a positive owner-earnings estimate.
8. Do not append both the new model and analyst triplet into one six-value distribution or auto-switch models opportunistically. First implementation would use an explicitly selected method-only research cohort so that the accepted three-value aggregation is reproducible and the analyst path remains separate. Selection, fallback priority or mixing would need a further explicit method-policy decision, not an implicit median change.

Sensitivity is transparent: a10% change in the current EPS proxy changes each estimate10% if the historical cohort is fixed; choosing another percentile/lookback changes the anchor and hence entry levels; the current-vs-historical share basis and one-off earnings can dominate outcomes. Historical multiples can encode a bubble or a different rate/growth regime. This relative-pricing model does not independently establish intrinsic value, forecast growth, executable prices or future returns.

**Material effect and approval boundary:** adding this recipe can make previously ineligible instruments eligible and changes the economic origin of the V3 anchor. Supported provenance syntax alone does not authorize those choices. The user explicitly required approval before a semantic change. Therefore no estimator, percentile rule, new dispersion cap, source-route expansion or strategy selection was implemented in this execution. Approving the named recipe would authorize that bounded implementation/testing only; it would not initialize an account, connect an unaccepted provider or start PAPER. Rejection leaves this model disabled; legitimate external explicit estimates remain supported.
