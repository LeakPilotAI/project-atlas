## Minimum acquisition package and checklist
1. Operator identifies intended initial assets, source products and usage: offline research, local display, derived outputs, retention/backup, sharing or redistribution. Ask whether each is covered, what exchange entitlements apply, correction history availability, termination/deletion obligations and retention restrictions. An API key or paid account is not evidence all uses are licensed. Credentials are excluded from the package.
2. Name data custodian and independent reviewer. Record organizational/control relationships and who can alter payloads, timestamps and witness records. Two files or keys controlled by the same producer do not establish independent custody. Manual approval alone does not authenticate clocks.
3. Agree a bounded coverage schedule before evaluation: explicit UTC endpoints, instruments including identifier history, source/product, premarket/regular/after-hours scope where intended, timezone/calendar, expected observation frequency, revisions and known gaps. Cover every input lookback before each forecast, each complete 1D/5D target window, and corrections through the declared evaluation cutoff. No universal number of months or samples is approved; cohort adequacy must be predeclared by the research reviewer. Initial small intake samples test provenance only, not skill.
4. Preserve original licensed payload bytes, original forecast artifacts and all correction vintages. Supply an exact file manifest (relative names, byte lengths, digest algorithm/digests), schema descriptions and source-record mappings. Hashes establish supplied-byte consistency only. Keep confidential license/witness documents access-controlled outside public exports; no secrets or database payloads requested.
5. Supply publication and receipt evidence separately from observation clocks. Use timezone-qualified source-native precision with documented clock uncertainty. Do not fabricate microseconds from dates/seconds. If uncertainty overlaps an issue/freeze/evaluation boundary, historical availability is unresolved. A receipt collected today cannot independently establish availability yesterday without a credible historical archive attestation.
6. Supply witness originals and approved offline verification material/instructions. Possible categories are provider-authenticated delivery records, independently custodied timestamped archive receipts, or independently verified signed timestamp evidence; none is accepted merely by category. Reviewer must assess origin, binding, identity, trust/revocation validity where applicable and historical clock claim.
7. Supply a package-wide receipt and revision index, complete scoped history statement, gap ledger and exact source/cohort associations. Reusing public metadata is legitimate; reusing outcomes as independent samples or relabeling source vintages is not.
8. Keep training/model-selection/calibration/validation/holdout assignment and before-use freeze evidence separate. Stage20 selection/holdout declaration is insufficient to certify the other partitions. Independent reviewer approves partition and correction-as-of policy before outcomes are exposed for scoring.

## Verification sequence and rejection
Quarantine as unaccepted input without changing production state. Inventory and hash originals; verify exact manifest coverage; validate declaration schema; independently verify rights and witness proof; reconcile product/provider/source/asset/revision identities; check unique receipts and full chains; reconstruct historical availability under uncertainty; run existing read-only chronology/evidence tools; retain discrepancies and issue a scoped reviewer decision. Codex can check supplied bytes, declarations, deterministic reconstruction and contradictions offline. Codex cannot create legal rights, witness independence, missing historical records or true unseen custody. No source is accepted because a local validator returns structurally valid.

Reject or keep UNVERIFIED when license scope is missing/expired/incompatible; issuer trust is absent; payload/receipt digest mismatches; provider metadata conflicts; receipt identities certify conflicting payloads; revision links are missing/cyclic/conflicting; source vintage or cohort is substituted; cutoff visibility is late/ambiguous; expected coverage is incomplete; forecast publication is retrospective; declared completeness cannot be independently substantiated. Preserve missing/unfavorable observations and denial reasons. Do not repair evidence by inventing clocks or rewriting retained originals.
