# Stage 19 — independent local chronology review and AFI closure

Decision: **Phase 2 HOLD; Phase 3 blocked; production NO-GO.** This review is a separate local analysis path, not independent provider attestation. Official AFI-1 through AFI-10 statuses remain PENDING. The 21 release decisions and startup lock are unchanged.

`audit_declared_chronology` verifies existing forecast/outcome artifact chains, then independently examines issue, input-observation, claimed availability, retained-review generation, outcome cutoff and correction clocks without importing V2 rendering or score logic. It reports retrospective forecast reviews, outcome reviews generated after their claimed cutoff, and corrections whose claimed availability predates the prior outcome. It always reports `provider_publication_witnessed: false`, `local_receipt_time_bound: false`, zero authoritative scores and no production approval. Dataset manifests and local outcome files lack an independently authenticated provider-publication witness and a separately bound local receipt timestamp. An internally consistent chain does not fill those gaps.

Adversarial local tests cover publication before observation, publication beyond the accepted acquisition/evaluation window, missing publication time, a review that postdates its outcome, backdated correction publication, three ordered outcome revisions, source substitution, duplicate forecast cases and equivalent `Z`/`+00:00` UTC notation. Existing Stage 18 cases additionally cover cross-asset and model-version separation. Corrupt or mismatched source histories refuse through immutable history reconstruction. The audit neither reads protected PAPER journals nor creates/rewrites evidence. The retained V1 exact-byte reconstruction remains in the neighboring regression selection.
